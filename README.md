# Aura Roleplay Bots

Welcome to the official repository for the Discord roleplay bots developed and hosted by Aura Systems under the **Aura Roleplay (Aura RP)** brand.

Our goal is to provide an immersive, reliable, and high-quality roleplay experience for communities of all sizes. We are continuously improving our systems and are currently developing a dedicated bot dashboard to make configuration, management, and customization easier for everyone.

As part of our launch, all Aura RP services will be available **free of charge for the first two weeks**.
